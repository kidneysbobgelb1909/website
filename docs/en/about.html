About Us

Founded in 1994, Jiin-Tech Industrial Co., Ltd. specializes in the design and manufacture of precision stamping dies.

From precision die design and progressive die development through to volume production, we provide complete stamping solutions. Because die design, toolmaking and stamping all take place in-house, we respond quickly to customer needs and keep full control of quality.

ISO 9001 certified, we have long served the passive components industry, supplying parts for inductors, capacitors and other electronic components to leading manufacturers in Hsinchu's technology hub.
